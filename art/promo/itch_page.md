## 中文

### 方舟幸存者 —— 点亮灯火，穿过深海

带着一盏灯火，领着罗德岛的干员，在涨潮的深海里活到天亮。像素风「类幸存者」肉鸽：你负责走位，编队自动开火（也可以改成手动瞄准）。

**特色**

- **13 名干员，自由编队**：水月、斯卡蒂、铃兰、维什戴尔……开局选主控，一路招募成三人小队，每人三项技能随精英化解锁。
- **灯火就是生命线**：灯火越亮越强，暗下来敌人就更凶；找到灯标点亮它，把黑暗推回去。
- **10 位 Boss、4 个结局**：偏执泡影、最后的骑士、伊莎玛拉……预警清楚、破绽明确；262 件藏品、8 条流派，每局组出不同的打法。

**操作**

- 移动：WASD / 方向键；冲刺：空格（无敌）；手动技能：Q / E
- 攻击默认全自动；设置里可改为手动：鼠标左键 / J 攻击，朝光标方向
- 升级 / 藏品：数字键或点击选择；Tab / C 查看属性；Esc 暂停
- 手柄：左摇杆移动、Ⓑ / RB 冲刺、Ⓐ / Ⓨ 手动技能、Ⓧ / RT 手动攻击、右摇杆瞄准

## English

### Arknights Survivors — Light the lamp. Outlast the deep.

Carry one lamp, lead a squad of Rhodes Island operators, and survive the rising sea until dawn. A pixel-art survivor-like roguelite: you handle the movement, your squad attacks automatically (or switch to manual aiming).

**Features**

- **13 operators, your own squad**: Mizuki, Skadi, Suzuran, Wisadel and more. Pick a leader, recruit a three-operator squad, and unlock three skills per operator as they promote.
- **Your lamp is your lifeline**: a bright lamp makes you stronger, a dim one makes the sea deadlier. Find beacons and light them to push the dark back.
- **10 bosses, 4 endings**: Paranoia, the Last Knight, Ishar-mla and more, with clear tells and clear openings. 262 relics across 8 build lanes make every run different.

**Controls**

- Move: WASD / arrow keys · Dash: Space (invulnerable) · Manual skills: Q / E
- Attacks are automatic by default; switch to manual in Settings: left mouse / J to attack toward the cursor
- Level-up / relic picks: number keys or click · Stats: Tab / C · Pause: Esc
- Gamepad: left stick move · Ⓑ / RB dash · Ⓐ / Ⓨ manual skills · Ⓧ / RT manual attack · right stick aim
