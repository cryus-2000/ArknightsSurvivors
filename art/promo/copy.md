## 一句话 · One-liner

**中文**：带着一盏灯火，领着罗德岛的干员，在涨潮的深海里活到天亮。

**English**: Carry one lamp, lead a squad of Rhodes Island operators, and survive the rising sea until dawn.

## 卖点 · Key features

| 中文 | English |
|---|---|
| **13 名干员，自由编队**：水月、斯卡蒂、铃兰、维什戴尔……开局选主控，一路招募成三人小队（藏品还能多带一位），每人三项技能随精英化解锁。 | **13 operators, your own squad**: Mizuki, Skadi, Suzuran, Wisadel and more. Pick a leader, recruit a three-operator squad (a relic can add a fourth), and unlock three skills per operator as they promote. |

## 简介 · About (≈120 字 / ~120 words)

**中文**：深蓝之树开始生长，海嗣从潮水里涌上岸。你是罗德岛的一名干员，手里只有一盏会熄灭的灯火。这是一款像素风「类幸存者」肉鸽：移动、走位，攻击交给编队自动打出（也可以改成手动瞄准）；升级时三选一，招募干员、升级技能、收集藏品，组出属于你的流派。点亮灯标、打开祭坛、看清 Boss 的预警，在十几分钟的潮汐里撑到最后——然后选择，你要让这片海变成什么样。

**English**: The Tree of the Deep Blue is growing, and the Seaborn are pouring ashore. You are a Rhodes Island operator with nothing but a lamp that can go out. This is a pixel-art survivor-like roguelite: you move and dodge while your squad attacks automatically (or switch to manual aiming). Level up with three-way picks — recruit operators, upgrade skills and collect relics to build your own style. Light beacons, open altars, read each boss's tells, and hold out through a tide that lasts a dozen-odd minutes. Then decide what this sea becomes.
